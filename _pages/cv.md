## Research Interests

- 3D scene localization and navigation  
- 3D content editing and generation  
- Point cloud analysis  
- 3D reconstruction  

My research focuses on multimodal point cloud completion and generation, neural surface reconstruction, and language-guided 3D localization. These topics align closely with IEEE Transactions on Image Processing (TIP) areas such as image/video restoration and enhancement, machine learning for image processing, and 3D image/video analysis. According to [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-qDvzxAAAAAJ) (accessed Nov 2026): **1,029 citations**, **h-index 13**, **i10-index 15**. Publications appear in CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ACM MM, AAAI, IJCAI, ICML, TVCG, TCSVT, Information Fusion, ISPRS JPRS, and IEEE TITS.
